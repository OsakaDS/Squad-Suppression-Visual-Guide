The Suppression
Data

by Osaka [29th ID] · the asset-level companion to the field guide

Squad does not store a suppression stat on a weapon. It stores a profile that a bullet carries, a curve keyed on how close that bullet passed your head, and a ceiling on how much pressure can stack up. Every weapon, icon and number below was read out of the packaged .uasset files, no wiki figures. This is the data page; the field guide explains what it means in a firefight.

Stage 01

The armoury

Every firearm in the game, sorted by the suppression profile its rounds actually carry, not by what the weapon looks like. Pick one and the rest of the page follows it. Icons and category emblems are the game's own art, lifted from the texture assets.

Stage 02

Which profile your pick carries

Suppression is a property of the round in flight, not the gun that fired it. The weapon names a projectile, the projectile names a suppression profile, unless the weapon overrides that choice outright.

Stage 03

A round goes past you

The only thing that matters is the perpendicular distance between the bullet's path and your body. Range to the shooter is irrelevant. A sniper at 600 m and a rifleman at 40 m apply identical pressure if the round passes the same distance from your head.

Suppression added per round

Every curve dies at 5–7 m. Outside that a passing round does nothing at all, and the differences between classes live almost entirely inside the first two metres. Your selected weapon's profile is drawn thicker; the y-scale rescales when you add the DMR/Marksman or sniper curves, which open an order of magnitude above the rest.

Stage 04

Pressure stacks, then stops

Each passing round adds its power to a running total, clamped at the profile's ceiling, MaxSuppressionThreshold. A weapon's real advantage is how fast it pins you, not how far past the cap it can push.

Recovery is not in the assets. The rate suppression bleeds off once fire stops lives in Squad's compiled C++, which the mod SDK does not ship, so this models build-up only.

Stage 05

Explosions use a second, larger model

Blasts skip the passby path entirely: their SuppressionPower is zeroed. They suppress radially between an inner and an outer radius instead, with their own power, curve and ceiling. It is why one grenade outweighs a magazine, and why the 74 launcher weapons in the armoury show no passby figure.

Radial envelopes

Bars run from the inner radius (full power) to the outer radius (zero). A hand grenade's envelope reaches 22.5 m, four times a rifle round's five.

Reading the numbers honestly

  • Read directly from the assets: every power, ceiling, radius, sway figure and curve key, the weapon → projectile → profile wiring for all 904 weapon assets, the display names, and the icons, which are the content-browser thumbnails with the editor's checkerboard keyed back out to alpha.
  • Inferred: that the curve's X axis is perpendicular miss distance rather than range to shooter. The property is PowerToDistanceCurve, its companion is ObstructedClosenessMult, and a 5 m ceiling only makes sense as closeness. Also that SuppressionPower is the fallback used when a profile has no curve, which is how the shotgun and tank profiles are shaped.
  • Category emblems are the game's own role icons (rifleman, machine gunner, breacher and so on) matched to each profile. Squad ships no per-profile art, so the pairing is mine.
  • Not available: the decay rate, the exact mapping from suppression to screen effect, and the closeness-ratio normalisation all live in /Script/Squad, compiled C++ that the mod SDK ships without source.
